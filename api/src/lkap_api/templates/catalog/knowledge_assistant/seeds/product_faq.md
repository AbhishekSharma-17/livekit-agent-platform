# Acme Meter — product FAQ

Acme Meter is a fictional smart energy meter with a companion app. This FAQ is sample content for the Knowledge assistant starter; replace it with your own documents.

## Plans

Q: Which plans are there?
A: Three plans: Basic, Plus and Pro. Every plan includes the meter, the mobile app and email support.

Q: What does the Basic plan include?
A: Live usage in the app, a daily usage summary, one home and email support. Basic is free with the meter.

Q: What does the Plus plan include?
A: Everything in Basic plus hourly history for 24 months, bill forecasts, up to three homes and chat support. Plus costs 4 dollars a month.

Q: What does the Pro plan include?
A: Everything in Plus plus API access, data export, up to ten homes and phone support. Pro costs 12 dollars a month.

Q: Which plan includes API access?
A: Only the Pro plan includes API access.

Q: Can I change plans at any time?
A: Yes. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades apply at the start of the next billing month.

## Limits

Q: How many homes can I add?
A: One on Basic, three on Plus and ten on Pro.

Q: How long is my history kept?
A: Basic keeps 30 days of daily totals. Plus and Pro keep 24 months of hourly history.

Q: What are the API limits?
A: The Pro API allows 60 requests a minute and 50,000 requests a day per account.

Q: How many people can share one home?
A: Up to five app users per home on every plan.

## Billing and refunds

Q: What is your refund policy?
A: Paid plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of the first payment. After 30 days we refund the unused part of the current month when you cancel. The meter itself can be returned unused within 30 days for a full refund.

Q: When am I billed?
A: On the same day each month as your first payment.

Q: Which payment methods do you accept?
A: Credit and debit cards. Invoices are available on the Pro plan for annual billing.

Q: Is there an annual discount?
A: Yes, two months free when you pay a year in advance on Plus or Pro.

## The meter

Q: How do I install the meter?
A: A qualified electrician installs it in about 30 minutes. The app then pairs with it over Wi-Fi.

Q: The app says my meter is offline. What should I do?
A: Check that the home Wi-Fi is working, then restart the meter by holding its button for ten seconds. If it is still offline after five minutes, contact support.

Q: Does the meter work without Wi-Fi?
A: It keeps measuring and stores up to seven days of data, which it uploads when Wi-Fi returns.

Q: What is the warranty?
A: Two years on the meter hardware.

## Support

Q: What are your support hours?
A: Email support answers within one business day. Chat support (Plus and Pro) runs Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm. Phone support (Pro) runs Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.

Q: How do I reach a person?
A: Ask the assistant to hand you over, or use the contact form in the app.
