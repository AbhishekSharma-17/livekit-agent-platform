# Acme Dental — practice information

Acme Dental is a fictional practice used by the Receptionist starter. Replace this document with your own practice information.

## Opening hours

- Monday to Thursday: 8:00 am to 6:00 pm
- Friday: 8:00 am to 3:00 pm
- Saturday: 9:00 am to 12:00 pm, check-ups and cleanings only
- Sunday and public holidays: closed

## Address and parking

- 12 Example Street, Suite 3, Exampletown.
- Free parking behind the building; the entrance is on the car park side.
- Step-free access and a lift to the second floor.

## Services

- Cleaning (hygienist): 30 minutes.
- Check-up: 20 minutes, includes X-rays once a year.
- Filling: 45 minutes, booked after a check-up.
- Consultation: 30 minutes, for new patients, cosmetic questions or a second opinion.

## New patients

- Bring a photo ID and a list of current medication.
- Arrive ten minutes early to fill in the medical history form.

## Cancellation policy

- Please cancel or move an appointment at least 24 hours in advance.
- Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged a fee of 25 dollars.
- Reminders are sent by text message the day before the appointment.

## Emergencies

- For severe pain or swelling during opening hours, ask for the same-day emergency slot.
- Outside opening hours, call the local out-of-hours dental service.
