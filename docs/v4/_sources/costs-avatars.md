## Could not confirm

1. **Beyond Presence path discrepancy.** LiveKit plugin (`livekit-plugins-bey/.../avatar.py`, main) POSTs `/v1/session` (singular); docs and `api.bey.dev/openapi.json` say `/v1/sessions`. The two specs also disagree: `api.bey.dev/openapi.json` has `/v1/calls` + `/v1/sessions`; `docs.bey.dev/openapi.json` has `/v1/conversations` + `/v1/livekit-rooms` and no `/v1/sessions`. One is stale; could not tell which.
2. **Beyond Presence per-session correlation.** Plugin discards the session `id`; session objects carry no room name/token echo. Whether the Agents `call_ended` webhook fires for plugin-created S2V sessions is not documented.
3. **Simli pricing numbers.** No vendor pricing page reachable today. Third-party figures only (labelled as such, not used in the table): Spatius blog `https://www.spatius.ai/blog/compare-pricing-leading-ai-avatar-services-2026/` "~$0.01/min (estimated)"; search-result summaries mention ~$0.05/min PAYG and Trinity-1 "under 1 cent per minute".
4. **Simli id linkage.** Whether `HistoryRecord.id` equals the `session_id` returned by `POST /integrations/livekit/agents`, and whether `kind` distinguishes LiveKit-agents sessions, is unconfirmed.
5. **Tavus usage endpoint absence** rests on `llms.txt`, the two conversation doc pages and a truncated `openapi.yaml` fetch (cut off before the conversation GETs). The shutdown field name differs between the webhook doc (`shutdown_reason`) and the verbose-events example (`properties.reason`).
6. **Tavus "Business $0.26/min" tier** appears only in the Spatius blog; not on today's `tavus.io/pricing`.
7. **D-ID pricing** (plans, per-minute) — page text not retrievable.
8. **bitHuman** `/api/runtime-sessions` and `/api/billing` response fields; **Anam** analytics endpoints — pages listed but not opened.
9. **Anam per-minute rates on the docs side**: `billing-and-limits.md` defers to "individual agreements"; the numbers above are from the marketing pricing page only.
