# Acme — offer sheet

Acme is a fictional software company used by the Lead qualification starter. Replace this document with your own offer.

## Plans

- Starter: for teams up to 5 people. From 15 dollars per user per month.
- Team: for teams of 5 to 50 people. From 25 dollars per user per month, includes single sign-on.
- Enterprise: for more than 50 people. Custom pricing, a dedicated specialist and an uptime commitment.

## Pricing bands

- Under 1,000 dollars a month: Starter or a small Team plan.
- 1,000 to 10,000 dollars a month: Team.
- Over 10,000 dollars a month: Enterprise.
- Annual billing saves two months.

## Integrations

- Calendar, email and chat integrations on every plan.
- A REST API and webhooks on Team and Enterprise.
- CRM integrations on Team and Enterprise.

## Trials and demos

- A 14-day free trial on Starter and Team, no card needed.
- Demos with a specialist are offered to teams of 5 or more who plan to start within six months.
