# Carrier policy lines

Reference notes on the policy lines the mock carrier directory
(`packs.insurance_claim.policy_directory`) issues, for `search_knowledge` to
answer general "what does my policy line cover" questions during intake. This
does not replace `lookup_policy`, which verifies a specific policy number.

## Homeowners (HO-3)

Covers the dwelling, personal property, loss of use, and personal liability
for an owner-occupied home. Water damage coverage usually depends on whether
the water loss was sudden and accidental versus long-term seepage, and
whether flood (a separate policy) was the actual cause. A water backup and
sump overflow endorsement, when on file, extends coverage to water that backs
up through drains or a failed sump pump — ask whether that endorsement is
listed before assuming a basement flood is covered.

## Personal auto

Covers collision (damage from hitting another vehicle or object) and
comprehensive (theft, weather, animal strikes, and other non-collision
causes) separately, each with its own deductible. Bodily injury liability and
medical payments coverage matter most when anyone in either vehicle is hurt.
Rental reimbursement, when listed, pays a daily amount while the covered
vehicle is being repaired. A lapsed policy needs underwriting and human
review before any loss after the lapse date can be intake-processed normally.

## Renters (HO-4)

Covers personal property and personal liability for a tenant; it does not
cover the building itself. Theft away from the premises is usually capped at
a percentage of the total personal property limit. Most carriers require a
police report number before a theft claim can move to adjuster assignment.

## Single trip travel

Covers trip cancellation and trip delay up to their own limits, plus a
smaller baggage delay benefit. A claim usually needs the carrier's own
cancellation or delay notice, the original itinerary, and receipts for
prepaid, nonrefundable expenses; any refund, voucher, or credit already
received must be disclosed so the claim does not duplicate that recovery.

## Supplemental medical reimbursement

Reimburses out-of-pocket medical costs up to an annual limit, including
urgent care and emergency room copays. A claim needs an itemized provider
bill and an explanation of benefits or denial notice showing what the
primary plan already paid or denied.
